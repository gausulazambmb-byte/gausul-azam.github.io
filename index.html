About Me

I am an M.Sc. Thesis Student in the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology at the University of Rajshahi. I graduated with a B.Sc. (Honors) ranking First Class First (CGPA 3.94/4.00) and received the prestigious Dean's Award from the Faculty of Science for academic excellence. My core research interests lie in Cancer Biology, Translational Oncology, Drug Repurposing, and CRISPR-Cas9 Gene Editing.

Currently, at the Protein and Cancer Biology Laboratory, my research focuses on investigating the anticancer mechanisms of small molecules and assessing the therapeutic potential of repurposed drugs across breast, liver, and lung cancer cell lines. My findings have been published in peer-reviewed journals and accepted at prominent international oncology conferences, including ESMO and IASLC. I am deeply committed to advancing cancer therapeutics and making meaningful contributions to the field of oncological research.

Core Research Pillars

  • Translational Oncology: Bridging benchtop molecular assays with clinical therapeutic applications.
  • Drug Repurposing: Identifying novel synergistic combinations of FDA-approved drugs with chemotherapeutics.
  • Functional Genomics: Applying CRISPR-Cas9 gene knockout models (such as TP53) to validate drug action pathways.

Academic & Career Vision

I am actively preparing to pursue doctoral (Ph.D.) research in global laboratories specializing in precision oncology, molecular pharmacology, and genome engineering.

Key Strengths: Mammalian cell culture, in vivo animal models, flow cytometry, RT-PCR, and scientific manuscript writing.

  • Carrying out in vivo experiments using Swiss albino mice for comprehensive anticancer activity assessment.
