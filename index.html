About Me

I am an M.Sc. Thesis Student in the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology at the University of Rajshahi. I graduated with a B.Sc. (Honors) ranking First Class First (CGPA 3.94/4.00) and received the Dean's Award from the Faculty of Science for academic excellence. My core research interests lie in Cancer Biology, Translational Oncology, Drug Repurposing, and CRISPR-Cas9 Gene Editing.

Currently, at the Protein and Cancer Biology Laboratory, my research focuses on investigating the anticancer mechanisms of small molecules and assessing the therapeutic potential of repurposed drugs across breast, liver, and lung cancer cell lines. My findings have been published in peer-reviewed journals and accepted at prominent international oncology conferences, including ESMO and IASLC. I am deeply committed to advancing cancer therapeutics and making meaningful contributions to the field of oncological research.

Current Project

2024 – Present

Investigation of the synergistic anticancer effects of Eltrombopag Olamine and Doxorubicin in A549 lung cancer cells

Role: M.Sc. Thesis Student

Laboratory: Protein and Cancer Biology Laboratory, University of Rajshahi

Description: Investigating anticancer mechanisms of small molecules and assessing the therapeutic potential of repurposed drugs in combination with chemotherapeutic agents. The research involves cell culture, molecular assays, functional validation of TP53 involvement using CRISPR-Cas9-mediated TP53 knockout in MCF-7 and A549 cancer cells, and in vivo experiments using Swiss albino mice for anticancer activity assessment.
